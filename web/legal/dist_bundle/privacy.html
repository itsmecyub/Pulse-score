Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

PulseScore has no accounts and no tracking. We do not collect, store or sell personal information.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. There is no account, no sign-up, no email address and no name. We use no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no third-party tracking, and the app has no ads.

What stays on your device

The app saves your setup in its own private storage on your phone:

  • chosen language
  • whether the intro is finished
  • default league
  • pinned teams and matches
  • notification preference
  • whether Premium is active

None of it is uploaded anywhere, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What leaves your device

To show scores, the app requests match data from our score service and club badges from the data provider's image server. Those requests carry only what any web request carries — an IP address, a timestamp and the general device type — used to return the response and keep the service secure. We attach no identifier and build no profile.

Notifications

Your on/off choice is stored on your device. Push notifications are not enabled in this release. If added, a device token would be used only to deliver the match alerts you asked for, and you can turn them off in your phone's settings.

Services we use

API-Football for match data and badges, Railway for the score service, Vercel for these pages, and BBC Sport for the news link, which opens in your browser under its own policy. We share no personal information with them, because we hold none.

Children, control and contact

PulseScore is not directed at children under 13 and knowingly collects nothing from them. As nothing is tied to your identity, there is no account to close and no data to request or export.
